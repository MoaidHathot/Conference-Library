Official description

AI teams need to prove their systems behave responsibly, but turning policies, safety requirements, or product expectations into evaluations is still slow and manual. This talk introduces a way to translate natural-language requirements into testable artifacts: concrete behaviors, targeted test cases, evaluation runs, and evidence of where the system aligns or fails.

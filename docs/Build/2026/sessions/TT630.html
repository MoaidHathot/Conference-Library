Official description

How do you turn AI model capability into real product value? We’ll cover the essentials of working with modern AI models — from choosing the right model and shaping outputs, to grounding with data and running at scale. We’ll discuss practical patterns for prompting, RAG, evaluation, and orchestration, plus the realities of cost, safety, and reliability. Bring your use cases—this will be a hands-on, discussion-driven session focused on what actually works in production. Seating for this session is first-come, first-served.

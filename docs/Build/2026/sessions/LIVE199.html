Official description

The final moment is here. Three startups. One stage. This year’s finalists take the stage pitching their solutions as they compete to become the 2026 Imagine Cup World Champion, win $100,000 USD, and a mentorship session with Microsoft Chairman and CEO, Satya Nadella. This is where ideas meet execution and where student founders show what it looks like to build with scale in mind.

Summary

Overview

The Imagine Cup World Championship final at Microsoft Build featured three student startups pitching live for the title of 2026 World Champion. Selected from over 1,000 submissions, the finalists demonstrated working AI products in creator IP protection, food supply-chain freshness, and physical therapy access, before Copy Flag was crowned the winner with a grand prize raised to $150,000 and a mentoring session with Satya Nadella.

Key announcements

  • Copy Flag named 2026 Imagine Cup World Champion [00:16:24m05sm10sp05sp10s] — Patrick Brown's AI defense platform for creators won the top prize.
  • Grand prize increased from $100,000 to $150,000 [00:16:07m05sm10sp05sp10s] — Microsoft surprised the finalists by raising the award on stage.
  • Mentoring session with Satya Nadella [00:02:55m05sm10sp05sp10s] — The champion receives a one-on-one mentorship with Microsoft's Chairman and CEO.
  • Three finalists selected from over 1,000 submissions [00:02:21m05sm10sp05sp10s] — Copy Flag, Spoilsafe, and Revora Health advanced to the world championship.

Topics covered

  • AI-modified copyright infringement and detection for independent creators and small businesses
  • Content fingerprinting, parallel signal detection (AI remix detection, semantic checks, background swap), and predictive defense
  • Low-cost sensor pipelines measuring volatile gases, temperature, and humidity to predict food spoilage
  • Decision-layer AI that tells supply-chain operators when to keep, discount, or redistribute inventory
  • AI-driven matching of injured patients with physical therapists, and accessibility (voice, text, gesture, foreign language)
  • Building trust in regulated industries like healthcare from the ground up
  • Recognizing the customer insight that triggers a pivot — from selling data to selling outcomes
  • The founder journey from victim of a problem to builder of a solution

Official description

What if every person on your team had a dozen competent coworkers available around the clock, across every function, ready to support them regardless of the task? The shift from copilots to coworkers is already underway. Napster Chief Technology and Product Officer Edo Segal will offer a look at what happens when AI actually shows up as part of the team. These multimodal coworkers combine video presence, real-time tool access, and relationship continuity to uplevel the employee experience. Seating for this session is first-come, first-served.

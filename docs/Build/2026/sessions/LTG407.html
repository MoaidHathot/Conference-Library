Official description

In this lightning talk, we demonstrate how to transform fragmented customer experience data into production-ready AI agents—at enterprise scale. Starting with customer feedback as the source of truth, we convert raw signals into structured intelligence on Fabric—powering real-time, agent-driven insights and actions directly in the flow of work. Built for production, with Entra security, DLP, guardrails, and end-to-end telemetry seamlessly integrated.

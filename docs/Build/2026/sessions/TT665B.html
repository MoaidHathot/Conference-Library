Official description

AI is everywhere—but scaling it across organizations is still hard. In this roundtable, we’ll move beyond hype to discuss the real challenges developers face when helping organizations adopt AI—from integrating into existing architectures and workflows to managing risk, governance, and change. Join peers to share lessons learned, explore what’s working (and what isn’t), and uncover practical approaches to: • Moving from pilots to production • Enabling responsible and secure AI adoption • Driving alignment between engineering, business, and customers This is an interactive session designed for developers actively shaping how AI is implemented—both inside their organizations and the customers they serve.

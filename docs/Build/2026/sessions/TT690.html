Official description

Explore what it means to shift accessibility left in your engineering pipeline so innovation scales inclusion by default. Learn how tools like GitHub's Accessibility Scanner bring accessibility signals directly into developer workflows alongside security and quality. We’ll open the discussion to share perspectives on what’s working, where gaps remain, and how you and your teams can embed accessibility into AI tooling and engineering practices.

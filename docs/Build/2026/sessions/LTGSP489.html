Official description

We have data showing AI agents are improving at a super-exponential rate. If this continues, we'll likely have agents 10x more capable than the ones we had in November 2025 — within a year. In this talk, we'll cover three myths about AI-Native transformations and how Augment transformed itself over the last three months. We've been blogging openly about this journey. You'll get the whole story and the learnings — so you can do the same for your organization.

Official description

What's changed in cloud infrastructure architecture and builds in the era of AI? Given that AI is rapidly changing everyday life in both consumer and business sectors, there are massive shifts occurring in cloud-based systems as well. In this talk, we'll explore how the patterns, principles, and pieces have changed—or stayed the same—for hosting scalable, production-ready systems in 2026.

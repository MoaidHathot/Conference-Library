Official description

Agentic workflows combine APIs, AI reasoning, and human decisions across systems—introducing new challenges in orchestration, reliability, and observability at scale. In this table talk, we’ll explore how teams are designing agentic business processes, comparing approaches to combining deterministic workflows and AI-driven decisioning.

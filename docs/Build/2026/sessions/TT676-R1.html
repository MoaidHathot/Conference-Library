Official description

Are multi-agent systems a breakthrough or architectural debt? While the hype is high, many teams over-engineer their AI stacks, leading to increased cost, latency, and complexity. This Table Talk focuses on when multi-agent architectures add real value and when simpler patterns are the better choice.

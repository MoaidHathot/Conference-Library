Official description

Learn how to build, publish, and monetize apps and agents with Microsoft Marketplace to reach millions of customers and drive scalable growth. Getting published on Marketplace surfaces your solutions in the Marketplace storefront and Microsoft products, such as Microsoft 365 Copilot and Azure, to reach customers directly in the flow of work. This lightning talk covers the start-to-finish publishing process and highlights benefits and incentives available from Microsoft for software developers.

Official description

In just 15 minutes, see how Copilot Studio can deliver a complete autonomous agentic automation —from idea to deployment. Learn to use a combination of out of the box and your own custom MCPs, leverage Copilot Studio’s both goal-based automations like orchestration and CUA as well as rule-based automation capabilities like Agent Flows. This session reveals how to streamline tasks, reduce manual effort, and supercharge productivity for developers wanting to modernize their internal systems.

Official description

Agents ship across frameworks and clouds—but telemetry is often fragmented. Let’s map an observability plan: what to trace (model + tool calls), what to measure (latency, cost, quality), and how to optimize (using trace-linked insights). Bring your scenario; we’ll compare a few viable patterns, debate tradeoffs, and leave with a practical checklist of next actions for your stack. Seating for this session is first-come, first-served.

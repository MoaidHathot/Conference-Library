Official description

As AI agents take on more planning, execution, and coordination, human contribution is shifting—not disappearing. This session explores what remains uniquely human in an agentic world, and why those capabilities will increase in value. We’ll examine where human judgment outperforms machine optimization, how trust is built in AI-mediated systems, and why connection and accountability can’t be fully virtualized.

AI workloads don’t behave like traditional APIs—they span models, tools, and agents across providers, making routing, cost control, safety, and observability harder in production. In this table talk, we’ll explore how teams are designing AI gateway patterns, comparing approaches for multi-model routing, policy enforcement, and cost management.

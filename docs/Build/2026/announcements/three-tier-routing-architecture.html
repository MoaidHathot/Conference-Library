Concept

Three-tier routing architecture

Route simple/medium prompts to an on-device model (≤13B), medium-to-high tasks to an on-prem workstation (14B–34B, with up to ~100–120B on larger boxes), and only complex/security work to the cloud (70B+).

About

A three-tier routing model distributes AI workloads by complexity: simple-to-medium queries (about 30% of workload, scored low on a 1-4 complexity scale) run locally on a Snapdragon X2 Elite, medium-to-high tasks go to a more capable on-prem workstation or headless machine, and the remaining complex 20-30% that the X2 Elite cannot run are sent to the cloud. The tiers map to your personal/desk computer, an on-prem workstation, and the cloud. The speaker frames this as a cost-saving distribution (contrasting with someone spending $1,000,000 a month) that can justify on-premises hardware to a CIO.

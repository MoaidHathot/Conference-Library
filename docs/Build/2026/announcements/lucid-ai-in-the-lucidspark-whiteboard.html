About

Lucid AI in the Lucidspark whiteboard tool focuses on idea generation, starting with the Mindmap widget that can expand on ideas from a topic and keep branching into sub-topics. The Lucid AI Brainstorm tool's Generate Ideas section can produce sets of sticky notes and generate further ideas based on a selected note. Users can also take existing sticky notes and summarize them or sort them into high-level categories using the Sort tool.

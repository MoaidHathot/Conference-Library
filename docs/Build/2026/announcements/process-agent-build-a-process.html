Process agent ("Build A Process")

A new conversational agent that ingests text, files, or voice-to-text, asks clarifying questions, and builds a first-draft flowchart with swim lanes.

About

Lucid's brand new process agent, available from the AI jumping-off point under "Build A Process," offers a more conversational experience than traditional Lucid AI. Users describe their process by pasting text such as a standard operating procedure, adding files, or using voice-to-text to capture a rough-draft prompt, and the AI fills in the gaps. The process agent works through the supplied information, may ask clarifying questions about forgotten details, and helps generate a well-thought-out flowchart along with a summary of what it has done.

Powered by 5th-gen AMD EPYC Turin, up to 35% CPU improvement, in 11 regions with broader expansion in 2026.

About

Azure's AMD-based DA, EA, and FAv7 VMs cover general purpose, memory optimized, and compute optimized workloads, with options for local SSD and new constrained-core sizes for better cost control. They are powered by fifth-generation AMD EPYC Turin processors, delivering up to 35% CPU performance improvement over the prior generation, scaling up to 160 vCPUs and 1.25 TB of memory with boost frequencies up to 4.5 GHz. The VMs already live in 11 regions with broader expansion planned in 2026, and show gains including up to 25% for Java, 65% for in-memory caching, 80% for crypto, and 130% on web applications.

Time-correlated platform telemetry across IP blocks

Data from multiple drivers operating in different time domains is unified into one time domain, removing the burden of manual correlation previously required of tooling

1 mention(s) 1 session(s) First in DEMSP384 @ 00:05:13 1 external link(s)

About

A capability called unified telemetry puts data from multiple drivers—which often operate in different time domains—into the same, time-correlated time domain. It collects platform telemetry from CPU, GPU, NPU, and power (and can incorporate OS-specific data via ETW), and combines it with application-side software tracing through ITT (Intel Instrumentation and Tracing Technology), an open-source project used to instrument much of the Intel AI stack such as OpenVINO and oneDNN [inferred]. Previously, getting data from different drivers at different rates and time domains required a tool chain to align them manually, which was always a complex problem; unified telemetry, available starting this week, removes that burden.

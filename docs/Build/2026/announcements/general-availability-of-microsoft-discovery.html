General availability of Microsoft Discovery

The enterprise platform, introduced in private preview at last year's Build, has moved to general availability

1 mention(s) 1 session(s) First in DEM315 @ 00:04:47 2 external link(s)

About

Microsoft Discovery is an enterprise platform for what the speaker calls "frontier R&D," using agentic AI to let teams of AI agents execute the scientific discovery loop end to end while humans provide intent, direction, guardrails, and judgment. Introduced in private preview at last year's Build, it has now moved to general availability. The team shared Build demos including developing novel PFAS-free immersion coolants [inferred] and breaking down plastics using proteins for recycling.

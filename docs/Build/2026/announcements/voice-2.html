Voice 2

Most natural-sounding speech model yet, preferred in 72% of blind listening tests, with fine-grained emotional control, voice cloning from a few seconds of audio, and a sub-150ms Flash variant for voice agents

About

Voice 2 is Microsoft AI's latest speech generation model, distinguished by its naturalness through deep work on prosody—the rhythm, stress, and intonation that make speech sound human rather than like a text-to-speech engine. Its headline new capability is fine-grained emotional control, letting users tune not just what the voice says but how it feels (warm, urgent, conversational, joyful), available today in 15 languages with more coming. It also performs high-fidelity voice cloning from just a couple seconds of source audio. A Flash variant designed for voice agents with very low latency is also planned, and the model can be tried on the MAI Playground or in Foundry.

Cerebras collaboration on high-speed inference

Cognition is working with Cerebras on models delivering thousands of tokens per second.

1 mention(s) 1 session(s) First in BRK244 @ 00:22:41 1 external link(s)

About

Swix explains that for massively parallel agents you want them to finish as quickly as possible, and that Cognition has been working closely with Cerebras [inferred] on models (referenced as 3, 1.5, and 3C2 coming up) targeting thousands of tokens per second inference. He contrasts this with typical inference speeds of roughly 50 to 100 tokens per second, and references Talus/Talos demos reaching 17,000 tokens per second, predicting near-instant commodity-token inference is mathematically coming.

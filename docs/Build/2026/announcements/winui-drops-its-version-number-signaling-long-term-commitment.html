WinUI drops its version number, signaling long-term commitment

WinUI 3 will now be referred to simply as "WinUI" to convey there is no intention of building yet another new framework or a massive breaking change.

1 mention(s) 1 session(s) First in OD854 @ 00:02:37 1 external link(s)

About

In response to community questions about whether Microsoft is serious about sticking with the framework, the team announced that WinUI 3 (now four years old) will be referred to simply as "WinUI," dropping the version number to signal there is no intention of building yet another new framework or making a massive breaking change. The team is pushing to stretch WinUI to be accessible for any use case and is integrating it into the shell at a faster rate, with many first-party Microsoft features being built on top of WinUI. They emphasized commitment to moving WinUI forward and hope to see broader third-party and enterprise adoption.

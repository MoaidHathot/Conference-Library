Copilot-authored merged PRs rose from 7% to 62% in under a year

The team embedded AI end-to-end across reproduction, testing, fixing, MAUI-aware code review, and documentation, with data published per-PR

About

The .NET MAUI team embedded AI end-to-end across their open-source engineering pipeline, including a MAUI-aware Copilot code reviewer with domain expertise, Copilot custom agents for verification and learning from merged PRs, and agentic workflows that determine documentation/blog needs and even open issues and draft PRs. With humans still in the loop, this approach took Copilot-authored merged PRs from 7% to 62% in under a year, peaking in April. The per-PR data across the .NET MAUI repositories and supporting Android/iOS tools is published openly on agenticengineers.net.

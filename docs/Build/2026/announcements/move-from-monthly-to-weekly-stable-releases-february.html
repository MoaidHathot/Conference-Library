Move from monthly to weekly stable releases (February)

Adopted to keep pace with the rapidly turning AI space and to land smaller, lower-risk batches rather than large monthly drops

1 mention(s) 1 session(s) First in BRK204 @ 00:04:37 0 external link(s)

About

The VS Code team moved from monthly to weekly stable releases in February, driven by the rapidly turning AI space where features shipped to the daily Insiders build would be matched by competitors within two weeks while stable only updated once a month. Adopting AI increased their output (roughly 3X more issues and many more open pull requests since January), creating competitive pressure to release more frequently. Smaller weekly batches also reduce risk compared to landing large monthly drops, letting the team validate changes and avoid on-call incidents from big batches. The shift reflects a broader point that adopting AI requires teams to also change how they work and evolve their engineering systems.

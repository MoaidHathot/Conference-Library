Microsoft Scout

1 mention(s) 1 session(s) First in BRK209 @ 00:12:04 1 external link(s)

About

Microsoft Scout is an autonomous "Autopilot" agent, demonstrated in Japanese performing a business trip expense reimbursement task. It simultaneously searches M365 cloud data and local data to find files, analyzes them to extract reimbursement content, and enters the data into the user's usual expense application. The agent takes the lead but leaves the final decision to a human for safety and security, with Microsoft Scout uploading files and completing the settlement.

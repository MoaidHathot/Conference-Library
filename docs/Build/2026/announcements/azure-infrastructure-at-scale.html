Azure infrastructure at scale

500+ data centers across 80 regions; the Fairwater AI super-factory (Georgia and Wisconsin) runs near-zero-water cooling and the largest Grace Blackwell deployment; Maia 200 (live in Iowa and Arizona) delivers 30% better tokens per dollar; Cobalt 200 VMs enter preview.

1 mention(s) 1 session(s) First in KEY01 @ 00:20:28 1 external link(s)

About

Satya Nadella detailed Azure's infrastructure scale, noting it now spans more than 500 data centers across 80 regions—the most expansive hyperscaler footprint—with more capacity added in the last 18 months than in Azure's first decade. He emphasized end-to-end systems optimization from electrons to tokens and community-grounded principles like not raising electricity prices and replenishing water use. He highlighted Fairwater, the first AI super-factory spanning Georgia and Wisconsin, designed from the ground up for AI in close collaboration with NVIDIA, using a two-story architecture to densely pack GPUs. The transcript window does not detail Maia 200 or Cobalt 200 specifics.

About

Dave Citron maps out the seven models Microsoft AI announced at the morning keynote, spanning image, transcription, voice, coding, and thinking. Image 2.5 is the image generation/editing model, now #2 on the LMArena [inferred] leaderboard for image-to-image, with a Flash variant at a third of the cost. Transcribe 1.5 is described as the world's most accurate transcription model across 43 languages and 5x faster than rival models; Voice 2 is the most natural-sounding speech model preferred in 72% of blind tests with a sub-150ms Flash variant; Code 1 Flash is a 5-billion-active-parameter coding model shipping as default in GitHub inside VS Code; and Thinking 1 is the first reasoning model scoring 97% on AIME [inferred] and 53 on SWE-bench Pro.

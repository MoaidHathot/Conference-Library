MAUI Labs initiative

A vehicle for faster experimental releases including DevFlow, Essentials.AI, AI extensions, and new Linux/macOS AppKit/WPF backends

1 mention(s) 1 session(s) First in OD803 @ 00:34:31 0 external link(s)

About

MAUI Labs is a new initiative created as a vehicle to release experimental things faster, acknowledging that not everything will stick but inviting the community to try, contribute ideas, and collaborate. It includes DevFlow, a companion that lets Copilot see and verify the application to work more effectively, and Essentials.AI, which follows the .NET MAUI paradigm to use on-device AI services like Apple Intelligence, Gemini on Android, and Phi Silica on Windows, plus local and remote models. AI extensions exploring new in-app navigation are also part of the effort.

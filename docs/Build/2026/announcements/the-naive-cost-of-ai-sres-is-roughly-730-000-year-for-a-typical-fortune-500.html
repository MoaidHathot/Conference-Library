The "naive" cost of AI SREs is roughly $730,000/year for a typical Fortune 500

At ~1,000 alerts per day and ~$2 per investigation (a midpoint within a $180k–$1.8M range), the cost often exceeds that of human triage and becomes a non-starter.

1 mention(s) 1 session(s) First in ODSP928 @ 00:00:43 0 external link(s)

About

Natan Yellin explains that a typical Fortune 500 sees at least a thousand alerts per day across teams, and running an AI SRE naively on all of them is too expensive. At roughly $2 per investigation and a thousand alerts per day, the cost rapidly reaches about $730,000 a year, a figure that matches what they see enterprise after enterprise. This is usually more expensive than having humans triage alerts manually, making it a non-starter.

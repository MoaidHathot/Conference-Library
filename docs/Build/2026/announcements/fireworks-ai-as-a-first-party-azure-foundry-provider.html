Fireworks AI as a first-party Azure/Foundry provider

Open-source models are accessible day-zero on Foundry with optimized, workload-aware inference served through the Fireworks stack

1 mention(s) 1 session(s) First in DEMSP383 @ 00:01:08 2 external link(s)

About

Fireworks AI is presented as a high-performance open-source inference provider with a first-party Azure integration, offering day-zero support for major open-source models (from Kimi to GLM 5.1) on Microsoft Foundry. Built by a founding team from PyTorch at Meta and Vertex at GCP, it serves around 30 trillion tokens per day and 180,000 requests per second. The Fireworks serving stack uses workload-aware optimization—adaptive caching, quantization, its own inference engine (Fire retention [inferred, likely FireAttention]), and hardware selection—to tune for ultra-low latency or high throughput. Users can access it via Azure and also bring their own models or fine-tuned weights to Foundry for serving.

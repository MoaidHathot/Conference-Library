Cache cooldown

Configurable window to keep compute warm and avoid cold starts.

1 mention(s) 1 session(s) First in OD813 @ 00:28:43 0 external link(s)

About

Cache cooldown gives you control over how long your compute stays warm between queries. By configuring the cooldown window, you can avoid cold starts and keep frequently-accessed workloads running with low latency, while balancing performance and cost—especially for bursty or intermittent usage patterns. Because scaling remains automatic, the system can scale up or down based on demand without manual intervention, preserving warm-query performance while staying efficient when workloads are idle.

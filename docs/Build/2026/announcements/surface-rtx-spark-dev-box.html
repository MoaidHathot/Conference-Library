About

Microsoft announced the Surface RTX Spark Dev Box, a developer "dream machine" that maxes out compute and memory on the new architecture. It delivers one petaflop of AI compute, 20 CPU cores, and 128 GB of unified memory access. It is coming in the fall, and attendees can join a waitlist. This follows the earlier Surface Ultra device that pairs NVIDIA power with Surface design, featuring 128 GB unified memory, a 2,000-nit display, and all-day battery life.

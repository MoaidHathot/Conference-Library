Autopilots and Scout

Enterprise-grade autonomous agents with identity and compliance; Scout is the first, available on Copilot Frontier.

1 mention(s) 1 session(s) First in KEY01 @ 01:44:21 1 external link(s)

About

Microsoft introduced Autopilots, described as enterprise-grade autonomous, long-running agents with full enterprise compliance that run in your tenant. Autopilots can have a name, personality, custom connectors, context, and memory, offering a new way to reduce toil. They join the evolving Copilot experience of Chat, Cowork, and Code converging into one Copilot super app. The first autopilot being introduced is Scout.

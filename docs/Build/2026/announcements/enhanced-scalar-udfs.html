About

Scalar UDFs in Fabric Data Warehouse are evolving to support richer, more complex business logic by bringing procedural constructs into SQL workflows. These include loops, multipath execution, and complex if/then/else branches. They are built for analytical scenarios and optimized for scale, letting you write cleaner, reusable logic without compromising performance.

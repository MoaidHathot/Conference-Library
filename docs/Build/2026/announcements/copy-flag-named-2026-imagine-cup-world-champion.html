Copy Flag named 2026 Imagine Cup World Champion

About

CopyFlag was announced as the 2026 Imagine Cup World Champion, selected over two other finalist startups. Founded by Patrick Brown, CopyFlag is an AI-powered platform that helps independent creators turn the invisible problem of AI-modified copyright infringement into a visible, actionable one. The win was announced by Hans Yang alongside Dona Sarkar.

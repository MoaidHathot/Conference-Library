Global XAML namespaces and implicit namespaces

Reduce or eliminate XMLNS declarations, with editor/language-service support now available

1 mention(s) 1 session(s) First in OD803 @ 00:16:53 3 external link(s)

About

As part of a big XAML investment in .NET 10, global namespaces were introduced so developers can reduce or completely eliminate XMLNS declarations in their projects, making XAML documents much less verbose. Implicit namespaces are in preview, and the language service and editor support that was announced as coming at .NET Conf in November is now available. The team encourages developers to try it and report feedback.

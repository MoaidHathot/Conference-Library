About

Nemotron 3 Ultra is NVIDIA's largest and most capable open model to date, announced Sunday with broader detail shared in the session and general availability stated for Tuesday. It targets frontier reasoning and orchestration capabilities with 550 billion total parameters and 55 billion active parameters. It is positioned at the top of three Nemotron sizes (nano, super, ultra) and is expected to run on roughly two B200-class data center GPUs. NVIDIA emphasizes fast task completion, quick prefill and turn handling for agentic tasks, lower costs, strong performance across ecosystem agentic harnesses, and a fully open release including datasets and research.

Cheaper models help less than expected

Models such as DeepSeek V4 are roughly three times cheaper than Opus but less accurate, and often pull more (and wrong) data, eroding the per-token savings.

1 mention(s) 1 session(s) First in ODSP928 @ 00:03:26 0 external link(s)

About

Yellin explains that switching to cheaper models is a first optimization but helps less than people think. The baseline assumes Opus 4.7 (or 4.6), and Chinese models like DeepSeek V4 are nearly three times cheaper but less accurate on complex problems. Counterintuitively, the overall price often doesn't drop proportionally because cheaper models tend to pull more data and pull the wrong logs first, then retry, ending up using more tokens. For this reason they run almost exclusively Opus family models with customers and reduce cost in other ways.

Section-boundary classification splitting, coming July

Documents can be split on logical section boundaries, not just page boundaries.

1 mention(s) 1 session(s) First in BRK242 @ 00:28:46 0 external link(s)

About

Coming in July, Content Understanding classification will enable you to split documents not just on page boundaries but also on logical section boundaries. This addresses customer feedback that boundaries in documents often don't fall neatly on page boundaries. It builds on classification's ability to identify document types and break long documents or enterprise packages (like case files, application bundles, or tax submissions) into logical parts.

About

Elastic is natively available on the Azure Marketplace, deployed, managed, and built through the Microsoft Marketplace, allowing customers to go from zero to a production-grade AI search deployment without leaving the Azure ecosystem, without a separate procurement motion, and without managing additional vendor relationships. When paired with models from the Microsoft Foundry model catalog, such as Azure OpenAI or other catalog models, Elastic provides grounded, relevant, and trustworthy AI answers powered by your own data rather than just the model's training data.

Insider Risk Management support for agent-driven activity

IRM now tracks agent-triggered policy matches, generating alerts with full context just as it has for user-driven activity.

1 mention(s) 1 session(s) First in OD829 @ 00:09:00 1 external link(s)

About

Insider Risk Management (IRM), the solution within Purview, can now track and list risks for agent-driven activity in addition to the user-driven activity it previously supported. When an agent triggers a policy match, IRM generates an alert and provides a full picture to the security team, just as it did for user-risk-triggered policy matches. The alert detail shows the specific agent involved, the user who triggered the interaction, the risk severity, and the sequence of activities—including what data the agent accessed, whether sensitive content was present in prompts or responses, and whether any DLP policies were matched—helping security teams assess whether it is a real threat or a false positive.

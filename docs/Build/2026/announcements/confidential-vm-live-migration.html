Confidential VM live migration

Coming to Intel TDX, providing near-zero downtime for maintenance on confidential VMs.

1 mention(s) 1 session(s) First in OD828 @ 00:36:35 0 external link(s)

About

Confidential VM live migration is coming soon to Intel TDX with v6 confidential VMs, providing near-zero downtime for maintenance and upgrades. It eliminates outages and operational disruptions for mission-critical workloads while improving reliability and performance by transparently moving workloads away from failures and rebalancing capacity without customer intervention. A demonstration showed a confidential VM (CVM) migrating from a source to a destination server, with attestation and key exchange occurring and only a short blackout time before the workload resumed.

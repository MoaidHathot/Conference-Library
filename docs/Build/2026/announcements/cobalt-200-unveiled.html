Cobalt 200 unveiled

Azure's next Microsoft-designed Arm CPU, delivering up to 50% higher performance over Cobalt 100 and positioned as Azure's leading performance offering.

1 mention(s) 1 session(s) First in OD828 @ 00:00:40 0 external link(s)

About

Cobalt 200 is Azure's next Microsoft-designed Arm CPU and the most exciting item on the Cobalt roadmap, following the strong customer reception of Cobalt 100. It is positioned as Azure's leading performance offering, with a new VM family taking shape around it. Cobalt is described as Azure's all-silicon CPU purpose-built for the cloud, reshaping the price-performance conversation; Cobalt 100 delivered up to 2x the .NET performance of the prior generation and up to 20% lower cost than comparable Azure VMs.

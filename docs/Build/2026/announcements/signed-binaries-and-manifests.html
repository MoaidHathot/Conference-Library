Signed binaries and manifests

Work with the .NET releases teams to sign all binaries and manifests for provenance and supply-chain safety.

1 mention(s) 1 session(s) First in OD804 @ 00:09:42 2 external link(s)

About

To bring the central management, auditability, and safety that system-level installers (like the one Visual Studio uses) have to user-level installs, the team has been working with the .NET releases teams to sign all of the binaries and all of the manifests involved. This provenance ensures the binaries you use are the ones the team intended to deliver, all the way down. The team also built a library around the tool so other tools that interact with installs can do so correctly and safely, with the hope that the manifests and bookkeeping become a shared resource for tools like Dependabot and Renovate and broader compliance infrastructure.

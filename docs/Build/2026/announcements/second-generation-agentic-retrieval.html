About

Foundry IQ includes a built-in agentic retrieval pipeline that performs query planning—deciding which data sources to look at and how to decompose the query—then runs searches, evaluates results, and iterates based on the configured retrieval effort before producing a final result for the agent. The team is now on the second generation of agentic retrieval, having significantly revamped the stack to take advantage of newer language models' improved tool-calling capabilities since around October of the prior year. Charts showed material improvements across a composition of metrics including recall, answer correctness, completeness, and reduced false "don't know" responses, compared against lexical (BM25) indexes, hybrid (vector plus lexical) search, and a minimal mode with agentic retrieval off. The trade-off is latency, since agentic retrieval can take several seconds compared to instantaneous search indexes.

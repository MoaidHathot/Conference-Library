About

Autopilot agents are a new, third type of agent in Foundry, joining the existing assistive and autonomous agent categories. Unlike assistive agents (which act as an extension of you) and autonomous agents (which act on their own behalf but can't use work IQ-style user actions), Autopilot agents always act on their own behalf and have a real user account. This gives them their own email address alias and the ability to send Teams messages and create Word documents on their own behalf, so they can perform actions that typically require a user account. They were demoed briefly in the prior day's keynote, and Scout in Foundry is cited as one example of an out-of-the-box Autopilot agent.

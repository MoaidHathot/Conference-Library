Chronicle session history feature

Indexes all of a user's Copilot sessions across clients to provide queryable, actionable guidance such as tips, stand-up reports, custom instruction improvements, and cost/token usage reduction

1 mention(s) 1 session(s) First in DEM305 @ 00:19:46 1 external link(s)

About

Chronicle builds a queryable history from every Copilot session you run, letting you take actionable steps to improve how you work with Copilot—such as creating stand-up updates, getting tips, and improving custom instructions. It can be run in the desktop app, in the CLI, through VS Code, through JetBrains, and on github.com. Chronicle indexes all of your sessions specific to you across every place you create Copilot sessions.

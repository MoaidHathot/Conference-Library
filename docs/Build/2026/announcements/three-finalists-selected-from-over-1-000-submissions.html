About

Dona Sarkar announced that three startups rose to the top from over 8,000 submissions to advance to the Imagine Cup World Championship, where they pitched to a panel of judges. This year's journey was shaped by Microsoft for Startups along with partners at GitHub Education and Replit, and founders were matched with industry-leading mentors. The judges were Dr. Irina Raicu, Bakari Brock, and Thea Myhrvold.

MRC (Multi-Path Reliable Connection)

A new Ethernet-based networking protocol co-developed with OpenAI that packet-sprays across independent "planes" to survive congestion and link failures during massive training jobs

1 mention(s) 1 session(s) First in BRK226 @ 00:07:00 0 external link(s)

About

MRC (Multi-Path Reliable Connection) is a new Ethernet-based networking protocol developed with OpenAI and a growing consortium of partners, replacing RoCE (RDMA over Converged Ethernet) in the Fairwater data centers that switched from InfiniBand to Ethernet. It is designed to be resilient during massive synchronous training jobs connecting up to 100,000 GPUs, where congestion or a single failure could otherwise force a costly restart from a checkpoint. MRC sprays packets across independent network domains called planes, so when a link is dropped the spraying rebalances across the remaining available planes. A demo showed two servers sending 4 GB payloads over 4 switches, achieving roughly 95 GB/s by spraying across 8 planes at about 12 GB per plane.

About

The session wraps up by summarizing three simple steps to become an MVP: be an expert, do what you love, and let the program know. To be awarded MVP status, you have to be nominated either by a Microsoft employee or by an existing MVP. The closing advice is to do what you love, share that, and contribute to the community.

About

Publishing to Microsoft 365 Teams and Copilot is generally available soon, allowing developers to publish all their Foundry agents directly into Teams and Copilot where teams already work, with identity, policy, and permissions flowing through automatically. In addition, developers can now publish Foundry agents as Autopilot agents into Teams, now in public preview. These Autopilot agents can take on their own IDs, email addresses, and even Teams presence so they can initiate conversations or follow up on action items, all governed end to end in Agent 365.

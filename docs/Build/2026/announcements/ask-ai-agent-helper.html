About

Ask AI, also referred to as the agent helper, is an in-portal assistant in Foundry for when developers get stuck and need more information. In the demo it is used to ask what a metric like coherence means and what a rating of four out of five implies. It looks at the developer's project and state, then returns explanatory information along with links to the documentation, acting like an expert chef in the test kitchen.

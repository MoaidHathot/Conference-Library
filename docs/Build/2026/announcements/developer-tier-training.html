Developer tier training

A spot/low-priority SKU offering training at 50% off standard-tier cost, with the median supervised fine-tuning job costing about a dollar

1 mention(s) 1 session(s) First in BRK231 @ 00:18:21 0 external link(s)

About

Developer tier (developer preview) is a training SKU selectable as a training type when starting a supervised fine-tuning run, allowing training at half the cost of a standard-tier job. It runs on a low-priority VM, so jobs may take longer, but it is well-suited for developers worried about fine-tuning being expensive. The training type options also govern data residency and cost.

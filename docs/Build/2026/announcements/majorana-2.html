Majorana 2

Next-generation topological qubit with a 20-second-to-one-minute mean lifetime (~1,000x Majorana 1) and one-microsecond operations.

1 mention(s) 1 session(s) First in KEY01 @ 02:19:04 1 external link(s)

About

Satya Nadella announced Majorana 2, the next-generation topological qubit implementing a new material stack discovered, built, and fabricated with the help of Microsoft's discovery agentic loop. Its qubits are exceptionally reliable, providing a mean lifetime of 20 seconds up to a minute—about a thousand times higher than Majorana 1—while operations run in one microsecond, enabling complex quantum computation within that lifetime. It retains the same qubit form factor as Majorana 1 at one one-hundredth of a millimeter, controlled all digitally, making it possible to fit a million qubits on a chip smaller than a credit card. With Majorana 1 having proven the foundational physics, Majorana 2 begins the engineering scale phase.

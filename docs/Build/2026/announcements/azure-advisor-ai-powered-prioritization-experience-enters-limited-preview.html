Azure Advisor AI-powered prioritization experience enters limited preview

Collapses hundreds of recommendations into a top-five prioritized, exportable action plan ranked by blast radius, criticality, deadlines, and cost; sign-ups offered after the session

1 mention(s) 1 session(s) First in BRK228 @ 00:42:53 1 external link(s)

About

Azure Advisor's AI-powered prioritization experience collapses hundreds of recommendations down to a top-five list with an action plan specific to the customer's environment. Microsoft plans to refine the experience further and bring the resiliency flows and advisor flows closer together for seamless end-to-end resiliency management. The experience started limited preview the day of the session, with a few sign-up spots available by meeting the presenters after the session.

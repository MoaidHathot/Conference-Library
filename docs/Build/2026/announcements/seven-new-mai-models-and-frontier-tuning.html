Seven new MAI models and Frontier Tuning

MAI Image-2.5, Transcribe-1.5, Voice-2, Thinking-1, and Code-1-Flash, with a Mayo Clinic partnership to co-develop a healthcare frontier model.

1 mention(s) 1 session(s) First in KEY01 @ 01:50:10 0 external link(s)

About

MAI announced a family of seven new models spanning Image, Voice, Transcription, and Coding, tuned for practical, efficient real-world use. MAI Image-2.5 and its Flash variant deliver a step change in quality, reaching number two on the leaderboard and surpassing Nano Banana 2 on image editing; they are live in PowerPoint, rolling out to OneDrive, and available on Foundry. MAI Transcribe-1.5 is positioned as the best transcription model in the world with state-of-the-art accuracy across 43 languages, beating Gemini and OpenAI flagship models and running five times faster, integrated into GitHub, Teams, and Copilot. The announcement frames these within MAI's pursuit of humanist superintelligence.

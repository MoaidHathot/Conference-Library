About

Planning in Fabric, a workload announced about three months ago in preview, is now generally available and will roll out to the rest of the regions over the next couple of weeks. It is an end-to-end enterprise planning tool that allows teams to manage budgets, forecasts, scenarios, and targets, serving as the source of truth for what should happen in the future. It is fully integrated with Fabric across the experience, security model, and OneLake configuration, with write-back to Fabric SQL databases.

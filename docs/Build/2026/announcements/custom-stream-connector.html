About

The Custom Stream Connector, currently in private preview, lets developers build their own connector, upload the package, and have Fabric host that connector to bring data from their own sources into an Eventstream. It addresses the need for developers who don't want to be limited to the connectors provided out of the box and have their own data sources. It complements the broader Eventstream capabilities such as SQL semantics, multiple destinations, and developer productivity features.

Capacity surge protection with mission-critical workspaces

Workspace- and capacity-level surge caps, plus marking workspaces mission-critical to exempt them from throttling

1 mention(s) 1 session(s) First in OD816 @ 00:08:52 2 external link(s)

About

Fabric capacity admins can apply workspace-level surge protection to cap ad hoc reporting workspaces (e.g. at 25% of capacity) and enable capacity-level surge protection that rejects background operations above a threshold to preserve room for interactive reports. Critical workspaces, such as sales during year-end close, can be marked mission-critical so they are exempt from the surge cap and get whatever capacity they need. This provides proactive guardrails that keep priority dashboards responsive while limiting noisy-neighbor workloads.

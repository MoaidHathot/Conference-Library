OneLake catalog natively integrated into Foundry

Trusted enterprise data, including sensitivity labels and endorsements, is discoverable and usable directly in Foundry knowledge and agents.

1 mention(s) 1 session(s) First in OD815 @ 00:27:04 1 external link(s)

About

Microsoft is bringing the OneLake catalog natively integrated into Foundry, letting users discover all of their trusted enterprise data in one place and immediately use it inside knowledge, agents, and any AI workloads. It contains the full capabilities of the OneLake catalog for discoverability, including sensitivity labels and endorsements, creating a consistent view of data across the entire estate of analytics and AI by making it available across both Fabric and Foundry. This data is made available without copying through a common data foundation, and can unify structured and unstructured data from sources like SharePoint and Amazon S3.

Voice mode in the CLI

Dictation-driven agent control, which Boyle uses with a hot-keyed remote to direct the agent while walking around

1 mention(s) 1 session(s) First in BRK200-R1 @ 00:15:59 0 external link(s)

About

Boyle notes that voice mode just shipped in the CLI, enabling dictation-driven control of the agent. He describes using it at home with a hot-keyed remote plugged into his computer so he can walk around his office with headphones on, clicking the remote to think out loud and direct his agent. He frames this as a highly productive way to work since he thinks while he walks.

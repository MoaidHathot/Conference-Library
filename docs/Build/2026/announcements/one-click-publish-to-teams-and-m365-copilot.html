Feature

One-click publish to Teams and M365 Copilot

From the Foundry portal, deployed agents can be scoped to the creator or submitted to the Microsoft Admin Center for organization-wide approval, yielding both Teams and Copilot UIs out of the box

1 mention(s) 1 session(s) First in BRK243 @ 00:35:35 0 external link(s)

About

Foundry offers a one-click publish to Teams and M365 Copilot so deployed agents can appear in the surfaces people use every day rather than as a standalone UI. From the portal, a publish drop-down lets the creator fill out a few key details—name and the actions the agent can perform, which is what end users see—and then make the agent instantly available scoped just to themselves or share it with the entire organization. The speaker demonstrates clicking the "Publish to Teams and M365 Copilot" button on a simple prompt agent and walks through the resulting dialog.

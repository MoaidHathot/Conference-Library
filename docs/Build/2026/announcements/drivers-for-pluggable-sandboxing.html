Drivers for pluggable sandboxing

Users can select the underlying isolation primitive (Firecracker, hardened containers, trusted computing, or gVisor/Kubernetes sandboxing) while OpenShell enforces the runtime on top

About

OpenShell introduces a concept called drivers that lets users pick the underlying sandboxing perimeter when launching it. Options include Firecracker, hardened containers, trusted computing, or SIG primitive sandboxing from Kubernetes [inferred: gVisor]. OpenShell relies on whatever isolation technology is chosen under the hood and enforces its runtime on top of it.

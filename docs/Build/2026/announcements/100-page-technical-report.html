About

Microsoft AI launched a technical report on its website the morning of the keynote, giving a detailed, over-100-page account of exactly how Thinking 1 was built from scratch. The report covers the complex architecture and training diagrams shown in the session and is organized around three governing principles: capabilities should be learned not inherited, simplicity is sustainable, and scientific rigor over shortcuts. It details data choices including the avoidance of open-source training sets and synthetic data, and the use of decontaminated benchmarks.

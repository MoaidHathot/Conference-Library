SharePoint and OneDrive shortcuts GA in OneLake

These join recently announced SAP and Oracle support, extending zero-ETL data access.

About

SharePoint and OneDrive shortcuts are now generally available in OneLake, joining the recently announced general availability of SAP and Oracle support. These shortcuts extend OneLake's ability to create a single pane of glass across all data with zero ETL, zero effort, and zero cost, regardless of where the data resides—on-premises, in any cloud, in databases, apps, or files.

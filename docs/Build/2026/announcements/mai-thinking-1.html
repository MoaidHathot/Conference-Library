About

Thinking 1 is Microsoft's first reasoning model, given an in-depth look in this session. Its architecture is a mixture-of-experts model with 35 billion active parameters, about 1 trillion total, and a 256K context window, and it punches above its weight class on benchmarks. It was hill-climbed entirely from scratch with no distillation and no teacher model, using clean, commercially licensed data lineage suited for shipping to enterprise customers. Training used 30 trillion tokens processed in house plus another 3.55 trillion tokens of curated STEM, math, and coding data in mid-training, which also extends context to 256K.

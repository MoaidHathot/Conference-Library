Union types are coming to C# 15, shipping November

About

Mads Torgersen (lead designer of C#) and Dustin Campbell announce that Union Types are being added to C# 15, which is coming out in November. They note the feature is already available in preview, with details on how to try it promised at the end of the session. They describe C#'s unions as their own "Goldilocks" / compromise version, distinct from unions in TypeScript or functional languages' discriminated unions.

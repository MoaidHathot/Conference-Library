Custom and out-of-the-box MCP tools

Developers can connect custom MCPs or use catalog MCPs to link local data sources alongside chosen models and RAG

1 mention(s) 1 session(s) First in OD839 @ 00:09:56 2 external link(s)

About

As part of the Foundry Local tools capability, developers can connect a custom MCP to their local data sources, combining the right model chosen via RAG capabilities with custom MCPs and other local tools. This combination runs on an Arc-enabled Kubernetes environment across the different form factors of Azure Local, from single-node to multi-node. The platform also allows discovering and deploying curated catalog models or bringing your own model (for example a container with models from Hugging Face such as YOLO 10 [inferred] or other predictive or generative AI), merged with your own OCI registry and served via vLLM or ONNX Runtime.

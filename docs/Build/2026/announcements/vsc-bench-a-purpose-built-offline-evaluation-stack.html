VSC bench, a purpose-built offline evaluation stack

Runs many daily evaluations of models and prompts against custom cases, plotting total tokens against resolution rate to inform reasoning-effort and shipping decisions

1 mention(s) 1 session(s) First in BRK204 @ 00:23:16 0 external link(s)

About

VSC bench [inferred] (heard as "BSE bench") is the team's purpose-built offline evaluation stack for VS Code, created because publicly available evaluation infrastructure and benchmarks were found insufficient for making objective product decisions. It uses the team's own cases and infrastructure to run many evaluations every day with different variations, measuring the impact of changes on metrics like total tokens and resolution rate. Sample plots of total tokens over resolution rate show trade-offs, for example that higher reasoning efforts (overlaid on the plot) do not always yield the best resolution rate and can cost significantly more tokens. These results inform pre-launch decisions about prompts and which reasoning effort to ship.

About

Transcribe 1.5 is presented as the best transcription model in the world, with state-of-the-art accuracy across 43 languages, beating Gemini and OpenAI's flagship transcription models on head-to-head accuracy benchmarks. Beyond benchmark accuracy, it is optimized for real-world use including noisy environments, accents, domain-specific terminology, and multiple speakers. On artificial analysis speed benchmarks it is up to 5x faster than rival models while also being more accurate. It is already integrated across the Microsoft stack—Copilot, Teams, GitHub, Dynamics 365—and is available on Foundry now.
